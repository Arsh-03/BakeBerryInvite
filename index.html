Crafting Sweet Memories in Belagavi

A New Milestone for Bake Berry Foods

❦

"What began as a quiet obsession with genuine butter, wild berries, and slow-fermented crusts has blossomed into Belagavi's most cherished baking tradition."

With boundless gratitude to the citizens and food lovers of Belagavi, Bakeberry Bakery & Cafe is overjoyed to celebrate the Grand Opening of our 4th Outlet.

Located right opposite DMart in Nehru Nagar, our newest branch brings you signature mastercrafted viennoiserie, artisanal breads, decadent gateaux, specialty coffees, and an ambiance made for sweet celebrations with family and friends.
